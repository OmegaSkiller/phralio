# Three directions for Phralio

Research and decision: 25 September 2026. All concepts are original constructions.

## 1. Fixed interval — selected

A stable vertical anchor sits between two quiet horizontal fragments. The visual
metaphor is attention holding steady while words arrive. A custom rounded stroke
wordmark complements system UI type. Petrol, mineral white and a separate dark
mint palette communicate precision without alarm. The mark remains legible at
small sizes and produces a strong opaque launcher icon.

Strengths: directly expresses the reading interaction, simple monochrome geometry,
quiet enough to coexist with native controls. Weakness: an abstract symbol needs
the wordmark until recognition develops. Keep it away from medical cross layouts:
the horizontal fragments are separated from the central stem.

## 2. Open margin

Two offset vertical brackets frame an intentionally empty reading space. An
editorial serif wordmark, warm paper, charcoal and muted brick would make the
product feel like a personal reading room. The icon would emphasize the gap.

Strengths: literary, expandable to long-form reading. Weaknesses: bracket symbols
are common in developer tools; the serif identity contributes less to RSVP's
fixation mechanism and introduces a separate font licensing decision.

## 3. Measured sequence

Three unequal rounded strokes increase in length around a common baseline.
A narrow geometric wordmark and indigo/stone palette would evoke cadence rather
than speed. The icon would be a compact sequence of beats.

Strengths: distinctive rhythm at large scale, flexible motion language.
Weaknesses: risks resembling audio/equalizer tooling, and small scale loses the
sequence nuance. Motion would add little to a reading surface that should stay still.

## Decision

Fixed interval is the clearest connection between identity and actual behavior.
It supports a stationary focal guide, restrained controls and native platform
navigation. No decorative animation is needed. The three rectangles and custom
wordmark paths were drawn for this project; no competitor assets or font outlines
were used. SVG masters are retained alongside generated platform artwork.
